
# AI Customer Support Chatbot

## Overview

This project provides automated customer support using AI.

## Problem

Businesses receive repetitive customer questions that consume time and resources.

## Solution

An AI chatbot answers common questions instantly, reducing support workload and improving customer response times.

## Features

- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) handling
- Instant customer responses
- 24/7 availability
- Knowledge base integration
- Automated support workflow

## Workflow

Customer Question
        ↓
AI Chatbot
        ↓
Knowledge Base Lookup
        ↓
Generate Response
        ↓
Answer Customer

## Sample FAQs

### 1. What are your business hours?

Answer:
Our business hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

### 2. How can I contact support?

Answer:
You can contact our support team by email at support@company.com or through our contact form.

### 3. Do you offer refunds?

Answer:
Yes, refunds are available within 14 days of purchase, subject to our refund policy.

### 4. How long does delivery take?

Answer:
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

### 5. How do I track my order?

Answer:
You can track your order using the tracking number sent to your email after purchase.

### 6. Can I change my appointment?

Answer:
Yes, appointments can be rescheduled up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

### 7. What payment methods do you accept?

Answer:
We accept debit cards, credit cards, bank transfers, and online payment platforms.

### 8. Do you offer customer support on weekends?

Answer:
Weekend support is available through our chatbot and email support channels.

### 9. Where are you located?

Answer:
We operate online and serve customers worldwide.

### 10. How do I get started?

Answer:
Simply contact our team or complete the onboarding form to get started.
